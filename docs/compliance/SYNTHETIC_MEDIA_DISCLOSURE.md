## 1. Regulatory Context & Statement of Purpose

Aster Companion provides expressive conversational interaction through cloud-first full-duplex speech synthesis. While Aster includes an array of curated, non-cloned synthetic character voices ("Warm voice", "Calm", "Energetic", "Playful"), the platform optionally allows users to customize their companion with **Consent-Based Synthetic Voice Cloning**.

Voice cloning involves the algorithmic extraction of biometric vocal timbre, pitch cadence, formant frequencies, and acoustic embeddings from recorded speech samples. Because vocal biometric identity is highly susceptible to malicious misuse (including financial fraud, family imposter scams, non-consensual deepfakes, and automated telemarketing abuse), Aster enforces an industry-leading, multi-layered regulatory compliance and technical safeguard regime.

## 2. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) & Federal Communications Commission (FCC) Safeguards

### 2.1 FTC Section 5 & Biometric Policy Compliance
Under 15 U.S.C. § 45 and the FTC's Biometric Policy Statement:
1. **No Deceptive Impersonation**: Aster strictly prohibits the cloning or generation of voices belonging to third parties without verified legal authorization. Generating a voice intended to impersonate a family member, colleague, financial agent, or public official is a catastrophic violation resulting in immediate account termination, model destruction, and law enforcement escalation where required.
2. **Prominent Truth-in-Advertising & AI Transparency**: The platform explicitly informs users whenever speech is synthetically generated. Aster will never represent itself as a human being or conceal its artificial intelligence nature.
3. **No Secondary Data Commercialization**: Voice recordings and acoustic model weights are never used for advertising profiling, foundation model training, or commercial resale.

### 2.2 FCC Declaratory Ruling (FCC 24-17) & TCPA Conformance
In February 2024, the FCC issued Declaratory Ruling FCC 24-17 confirming that AI voice-cloning technology constitutes "artificial or prerecorded voices" under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (47 U.S.C. § 227). In strict adherence:
1. **Outbound Calling Prohibition**: Aster companion voice synthesis engines cannot be integrated with automated dialers, PBX trunks, or outbound telemarketing robocall infrastructure.
2. **Mandatory Auditory Attribution**: In any scenario where Aster speech synthesis is bridged into a communication session (such as an authorized calendar call or screen share), an opening acoustic notification chime and automated verbal disclosure ("This conversation uses synthetic speech generated by Aster Companion") is rendered prior to user dialogue.

Voice cloning is locked behind a strict, four-step affirmative consent protocol managed by `ConsentLedger` (`synthetic_voice:*` scopes):

```
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. Cryptographic Proof Registration in Consent Ledger                  │

```

### 3.1 Randomized Liveness Verification Protocol
To eliminate the risk of an attacker uploading recorded audio files of a third party, the mobile application enforces real-time audio capture:
- The backend generates a cryptographically random 12-word phonetically balanced passphrase:
  *Example*: *"Silver falcon navigates rapid amber streams through mountain twilight sixty four."*
- The user must read the passphrase live within a 15-second window.
- The backend performs automated speech-to-text (STT) alignment and acoustic jitter analysis to verify live human speech delivery before accepting the sample for acoustic model training.

## 4. Technical Safeguards & Provenance Watermarking

### 4.1 C2PA Provenance & Cryptographic In-Band Watermarking
All synthetic audio synthesized by Aster's speech brokers incorporates Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA) standard provenance assertions:
1. **Metadata Manifest**: Audio streams and cached chunks package signed JSON-LD manifests identifying the synthesis engine (`Aster-SpeechSynth-v1.1`), the model ID, and the timestamp.
2. **Acoustic Watermarking**: Synthesized audio streams embed an inaudible, robust spread-spectrum watermarking signal. This watermark survives lossy transcoding (AAC, MP3, Opus), acoustic bandpass filtering, and ambient room re-recording, enabling deterministic forensic identification of Aster-generated audio in disputed incidents.

### 4.2 Sandboxed Execution & Confinement
- **Zero Export of Raw Model Weights**: Cloned acoustic weights (`voice_models` table) are stored in encrypted form with AES-256-GCM. Model files cannot be downloaded as standalone checkpoints (e.g. `.onnx`, `.pth`) by the client.
- **Profile Confinement**: A cloned voice model is strictly assigned to one companion profile. Profile B cannot invoke the cloned voice of Profile A unless explicitly shared via DEC-024 item-level sharing.

## 5. Revocation & Automated Cascade Destruction

Users retain the unconditional right to revoke voice cloning authorization at any moment:

### 5.1 Revocation Procedure
1. The user navigates to **Settings > Companion Studio > Voice Settings** and selects **Delete Cloned Voice**.
2. Revocation immediately invokes `ConsentLedger.revokeConsent()` for `synthetic_voice:model_training` and `synthetic_voice:synthesis_playback`.
3. Downstream listeners terminate any active speech synthesis sessions.

### 5.2 Cascade Deletion Guarantee (`cascade_deletion_worker.ts`)
Within 60 seconds of revocation:
- All raw biometric training audio files in the `voice-samples` cloud storage bucket are permanently deleted.
- All derived acoustic model weights in `voice_models` are purged.
- All temporary audio synthesis buffers in `media-chunks` are invalidated and wiped.
- An immutable `DeletionCertificate` with SHA-256 HMAC verification is logged, proving the total destruction of the biometric model without retaining any biometric remnants.

## 6. Prohibited Use Cases & Enforcement Policy

Aster Companion enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against deceptive or harmful voice synthesis applications:

| Prohibited Category | Explicit Violation Examples | Automatic System Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Impersonation & Deception** | Cloning public figures, political candidates, law enforcement, or acquaintances without written legal consent. | Instant permanent account ban, asset erasure, forensic audit reporting. |
| **Financial Fraud & Social Engineering** | Synthesizing voices to authorize bank transfers, bypass biometric voiceprint authentication, or execute caller ID spoofing. | Immediate service suspension, session recording freeze, referral to law enforcement / FTC. |
| **Harassment & Defamation** | Generating fabricated confessions, abusive voice notes, or non-consensual synthetic audio. | Account termination and permanent blacklisting of associated hardware device identifiers. |
| **Telemarketing & Robocalling** | Hooking speech generation into automated outbound dialing systems or commercial lead generation. | API credential revocation and notification to FCC enforcement bureau. |
